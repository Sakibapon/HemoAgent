Dialysis adequacy — previous session

Kt/V distribution (previous session) — KDOQI target ≥1.2
KDOQI 2015: minimum spKt/V ≥1.2 per session [2]. Under-dialyzed (Kt/V <1.2): 28.4% of this cohort.
Low albumin (<3.5 g/dL) in 40.2% of the cohort directly raises IDH risk — albumin is the primary oncotic force maintaining plasma volume during UF. Below 3.5 g/dL, plasma refilling cannot keep up with fluid removal, precipitating intradialytic hypotension. Reference: IDH study (Lee H et al. CJASN 2021) [3]; DOPPS [4].

Distribution of the 17 prescription values generated for all 500 patients using evidence-based clinical decision rules.

Blood flow rate (Qb) distribution — ml/min
KDOQI 2015: min 200 ml/min [2]. Catheter patients limited to 250. Qb increased if Kt/V <1.2 or URR <65%.
