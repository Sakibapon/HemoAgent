Reviewer details

All records on this site are synthetic. This review assesses whether they are realistic for a US in-center hemodialysis unit, in two parts: 20 treatment records (Tab 1) and 41 dataset statistics compared with acceptance ranges (Tab 2).

If left blank, the role is recorded as "Other".

Instructions (top right) describe each step with screenshots.

Review instructions

  1. 1Enter your details

    Enter your name and role (if you select Other, you may specify your role), then select Begin review. Your details can be changed at any time by selecting your name in the top-right corner. Responses are saved in this browser automatically; they are sent to the study team only when you select Submit responses.

  2. 2Tab 1 layout

    A Record list: 20 simulated treatments. B Chart, laid out like the hemodialysis synopsis screen of an electronic health record. C Assessment form for the selected record.

  3. 3Reviewing the chart

    Switch views with HD Synopsis, Flowsheet, Results and Patient. The graph icon plots a row across treatments. Rows marked calc are derived from charted values (for example, mean arterial pressure, UF goal and interdialytic weight gain as a percentage of dry weight); rows marked estimate rely on a stated assumption.

  4. 4Flagging an implausible value

    Select the value (1). It is highlighted in red and listed under 3 Comments (2), where you can state the reason. Select it again to remove the flag.

  5. 5Overall assessment

    Rate the realism of the record (1 to 5) and indicate whether it could have come from a real dialysis unit. These two items are required; the domain ratings, 2 Complications and 3 Comments are optional. After each response, the next item moves to your pointer. Each record takes approximately 1 to 2 minutes.

  6. 6Saving and continuing

    Select Save and continue. A check mark appears next to the record in the list. Next incomplete, below the list, opens the next record you have not completed.

  7. 7Tab 2, Benchmark comparison

    Each item compares a statistic computed from the dataset with an acceptance range. Part 1 (10 items) has no published benchmark, so your clinical judgment is the primary evidence; please respond to all of them. Responses to parts 2 and 3 are optional but welcome. Select Source to see how a statistic was calculated and how its range was set, then choose Agree, Too high, Too low or Not sure; the next item then moves to your pointer.

  8. 8Submitting your responses

    When you have finished, select Submit responses (top right, or at the end of Tab 2). Download saves a copy as a file. Clear responses removes the responses in the current tab or in both tabs, after confirmation.
